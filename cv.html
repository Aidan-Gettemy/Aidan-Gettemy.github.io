Profile

Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Texas at Dallas (advisor: Dr. Felipe Pereira), graduating February 2027. My research develops computational tools for high-dimensional uncertainty quantification, with a focus on Bayesian inverse problems, MCMC methods, and surrogate modeling, applied to fluid flow in porous media and wind energy. I have experience with large real-world datasets (SCADA), high-dimensional simulation output, and parallel computing on HPC systems.
